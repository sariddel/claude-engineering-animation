G-3 garage sequence

G-3 is a SpaceDIRT concept for a pressurized garage for SLED75-class rovers on a Moon Base node: a 4.2 m vessel with a curved plug door that moves in, lifts and slides up to the roof on tracks. The ramp tracks run out from channels under the deck. Geometry comes from the G-3 sizing record. SLED75, its front loader and its rototiller are drawn from SpaceDIRT CAD. Habitats and node follow NASA's Moon Base concept, with sizes scaled. Motion is not to time scale.
